# ENCORE by Hassan Allam Properties
## Red Sea, El Gouna

## Project Description

Encore by Hassan Allam Properties (HAP) brings Greek-inspired coastal living to Red Sea El Gouna, featuring a stunning 15,000 SQM crystal lagoon and sandy beach. This exclusive residential development offers 250 fully-finished units across 27 acres, with every unit enjoying direct lagoon views.

### Prime Location

Strategically located within El Gouna, Egypt's premier Red Sea resort destination. Part of the Swanlake El Gouna master development, with easy access to Shedwan, Tuban, and Tawila areas - all within 3 minutes drive (1.3-1.7 km).

### Greek Architecture

Features elegant Greek-inspired design with a gated compound layout. The development maintains a low footprint of less than 12%, maximizing open spaces and lagoon views. All buildings are equipped with elevators for convenient access.

### Unit Variety

Offers diverse options from 1-bedroom apartments (85 SQM starting at 19.3M EGP) to spacious 4-bedroom units with gardens (179 SQM + 84 SQM garden, up to 43.3M EGP). Select units include private gardens up to 87 SQM or roof terraces up to 71 SQM.

### Premium Finishing

All units delivered fully finished with A/C systems, kitchen cabinets, wardrobes, and porcelain floors - ready for immediate enjoyment of the Red Sea lifestyle.

### Crystal Lagoon Living

The centerpiece 15,000 SQM crystal lagoon with sandy beach provides a resort-like atmosphere. 100% of units overlook this spectacular water feature, offering residents a daily escape to paradise.

### Flexible Payment

**First time ever in El Gouna:** 7-year payment plan with just 5% down payment and 5% after 3 months, with quarterly installments. Attractive 22.1% cash discount available. Delivery in 2 years with HAP's proven track record of quality and timely completion in El Gouna.
