HAPTOWN - PARK 226 - Condensed Project Description

Park 226 by Hassan Allam Properties (HAP) is a modern residential phase within the HAPTOWN master development in Mostakbal City. Spanning 125 acres, this lagoon-centric community offers a nature-infused lifestyle with 62% of units enjoying stunning lagoon views across 3 acres of non-swimable water features.

Prime Location:
Strategically positioned in the heart of Mostakbal City, an extension of New Cairo. Just 5 minutes from the New Administrative Capital, 10 minutes from Madinaty, and 20 minutes from Rehab City. Excellent connectivity via Cairo-Suez Road, Cairo-Sokhna Road, and Road 90, with adjacent access to a Sports Club, retail zones, and a futuristic office park.

Modern Design:
Features Core & Shell apartments within 22 residential clusters, with an average building distance of 62-293 meters ensuring privacy and openness. The development prioritizes lagoon views and green integration with multi-purpose lawns, pocket parks, and jogging tracks throughout.

Unit Variety:
Offers 2-bedroom apartments (130-165 SQM) starting from 11.2M EGP, 3-bedroom units (176-198 SQM) from 16.2M EGP, ground floor units with private gardens (86-226 SQM) from 18.6M EGP, and 3-bedroom penthouses (240 SQM) with 85 SQM roof terraces at 24.7M EGP.

Premium Amenities:
Clubhouse, gym, health club, relaxation lounge, kids' play areas, F&B kiosks, 3-acre lagoon, jogging tracks, pocket parks, underground parking, 24-hour security, and integrated smart community features.

Flexible Payment:
8-year payment plan with just 5% down payment, delivery in 3 years, and an attractive 28.6% cash discount. Maintenance fees at 10% of unit price, backed by HAP's proven track record of quality and timely delivery.
