HAPTOWN - PARKVIEW - Condensed Project Description

Park View by Hassan Allam Properties (HAP) is a luxurious residential phase within the HAPTOWN master development in Mostakbal City. This tranquil community focuses on harmonious living with over 50% dedicated green spaces, artificial lakes, and modern amenities, all designed with smart infrastructure for contemporary lifestyle.

Prime Location:
Strategically positioned in the heart of Mostakbal City, New Cairo. Just 5 minutes from the New Administrative Capital (0.12km), 10 minutes from Madinaty (3km), and 20 minutes from Al Rehab City (15km). Excellent connectivity via Cairo Suez Road (7km), Regional Ring Road (6km), and Road 90. Close proximity to AUC (15km), Hyde Park, Mivida, and Cairo International Airport (35km).

Modern Design:
Features Core & Shell apartments in G+6 buildings with floor-to-ceiling windows for natural light and panoramic views. The development prioritizes a mix of water features and landscaping, creating a seamless indoor-outdoor living experience with modern layouts and contemporary finishes.

Unit Variety:
Offers 2-bedroom apartments (168 SQM) priced from 15.5M to 17M EGP, and 3-bedroom apartments (195-197 SQM) ranging from 16.6M to 19M EGP. All units feature modern designs optimized for natural light and ventilation.

Premium Amenities:
Swimming pools, gyms, spas, kids' play areas, high-end supermarkets, pharmacies, artificial lakes, extensive green spaces, walking and cycling trails, and 24/7 security with smart infrastructure throughout.

Flexible Payment:
8-year payment plan with 10% down payment, 15% after 3 months, and an attractive 23.6% cash discount. Ready to Move (RTM) delivery in just 6 months, with maintenance fees at 10% of unit price (after 6 months). Backed by HAP's proven track record of quality and timely delivery.
